## The three discount layers on a Chewy dog food order

**Chewy's own discounts.** The main one is Autoship, Chewy's repeat-delivery program. Your first Autoship order gets a discount that varies by product — brand pages currently show anything from 15% to 35% off, with a cap that also varies (some listings cap at $20, others at $50). After that, eligible items get 5% off on every subsequent Autoship delivery. Joining costs nothing, and you can skip, pause, reschedule or cancel a delivery from your account with no fee.

**Chewy+ membership.** An annual paid membership at $79 a year, following a 30-day free trial. It gives you free shipping with no order minimum, 5% back in rewards on eligible purchases, priority customer service and member-only offers. It launched at $49 and Chewy raised the price to $79 in late October 2025; the company has said conversion held up after the increase.

**Cashback through ShopBack.** This is the third-party layer: you start at ShopBack, click through to Chewy, and buy at the same price you'd pay anyway. The merchant pays ShopBack a referral commission, and part of that comes back to you as withdrawable cash rather than points. Cashback is funded by the merchant's marketing budget, so it doesn't change your checkout total.

## What voids your cashback (and what doesn't)

Three rules matter, and they're easy to trip over:

1. **Outside promo codes kill the whole order's cashback.** If you paste a Chewy coupon you found on a coupon site and it isn't featured on ShopBack, the order usually stops qualifying for cashback entirely. A 15% code on a $60 bag sounds great until you've traded away the rebate on the full order.
2. **Prescription food and pharmacy orders are excluded.** Prescription diets and treats don't earn cashback, and neither do pharmacy orders — that's a merchant-level exclusion, not something you can activate around.
3. **Cart state matters.** ShopBack's Chewy terms tell you to empty your cart first and start a fresh visit from ShopBack each time, then complete the purchase in that same session. Ad blockers, VPNs, in-app browsers and clicking other coupon sites mid-trip are all documented ways to break the tracking cookie.

Timelines are worth knowing before you panic: on Chewy, ShopBack says purchases track in about 2 days and confirm in around 75 days. If nothing shows as tracked within 48 hours, you file a missing cashback claim with the order number, date and amount.

## How much dog food you're actually buying

Cadence drives everything. If you're running Autoship, the interval you pick decides whether you're overstocking or scrambling. For a standard 30 lb bag of dry food:

| Dog size | Bag cadence |
| --- | --- |
| Small (10–25 lb) | Every 6–8 weeks |
| Medium (25–60 lb) | Every 4–5 weeks |
| Large (60–100 lb) | Every 3–4 weeks |
| Giant (100 lb+) | Every 2–3 weeks, or step up to a 40 lb bag |

A 75 lb adult Labrador on Purina Pro Plan Sport eats roughly a 34 lb bag every five weeks. At about $92 a bag, that's a bit over $1,900 of dog food across two years — which is the number that makes the 5% ongoing Autoship discount and cashback on every eligible refill worth bothering with. Chewy emails you three to five days before each Autoship processes, so a bad interval is fixable without cancelling anything.

If you feed wet food as the main diet, a 24-can case runs about every 3 weeks for a small dog and every 2 weeks for a medium one. Buy the case size that matches, not the one that's on the front page.

## Which route fits your dog

**You feed one brand consistently and you buy monthly.** Autoship plus cashback is the whole answer. You get the first-order discount once, 5% off eligible items forever after, and cashback on top of every eligible refill. It's the pattern that compounds.

**You order small, awkward baskets.** This is where Chewy+ earns its $79. Non-members pay $4.95 flat on orders under the threshold; two short orders a month is roughly $119 a year in shipping, which beats the membership fee. If your baskets clear $49 anyway, skip it and keep the cashback.

**You use prescription food.** Chewy's direct pricing and the 24/7 pharmacy support are still attractive, but budget on the assumption that no cashback is coming. Prescription diet and pharmacy orders are excluded at the merchant level.

**You're trying a new food.** Buy one bag as a one-time order. Burning your first-Autoship discount on a bag your dog refuses is the most common way people waste the best discount they get.

**You're already deep in Amazon's ecosystem.** Amazon's Subscribe & Save takes roughly 5–15% off subscription items, and it's available on Chewy's mainstream brands too. Where Chewy tends to win is the deeper first-order Autoship discount and the ongoing 5% on eligible items; where Amazon wins is a niche brand Chewy doesn't stock, or a same-day need. Cashback is available on both sides, so the switching cost between the two programs is low.

## FAQ

### Is Chewy cheaper than PetSmart or Petco for dog food?

On mainstream brands, usually yes, mostly because of the Autoship discounts rather than shelf price. Store-brand and private-label lines are where brick-and-mortar chains can undercut. Same-day pickup is the trade-off — Chewy ships in one to three days.

### Does the Autoship discount stack with coupon codes?

No. Chewy's first-Autoship and recurring Autoship discounts can't be combined with sitewide promo codes. They do stack with Chewy+ rewards and Today's Deals on eligible items.

### Can I use cashback and Chewy+ together?

Yes, and if you're a heavy buyer you should. Chewy+ rewards are paid by Chewy; cashback is funded by the merchant's affiliate commission and paid by ShopBack. Different funding sources, so they can apply to the same order. Cashback just won't apply to pharmacy orders or prescription food.

### How long before the cashback shows up?

Tracked in about 2 days on Chewy, with confirmation around 75 days, because the merchant return window has to close first. Once confirmed, it's withdrawable to a bank account or PayPal.

### Is there an annual fee or minimum spend on ShopBack?

No. It's free to use, with no subscription, no membership tier and no minimum spend — the commission model funds it. Which is why the sensible habit is boring: activate the click-through every time, empty the cart first, and don't paste a stray code.
